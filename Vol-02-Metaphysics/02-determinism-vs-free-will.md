# Free Will: Do You Have a Choice?

**Determinism:**
Cause and Effect rules everything.
1. The Big Bang happened.
2. It set atoms in motion.
3. Those atoms formed stars, Earth, and eventually your brain.
4. Therefore, every thought you have is just the result of a chain reaction starting 14 billion years ago.
**Conclusion:** You had no choice but to read this sentence.

**Free Will:**
We *feel* like we make choices. If I order a pizza, I could have ordered a salad. I am the captain of my ship.

**Compatibilism:**
The middle ground. Maybe we are influenced by biology and physics, but we still have enough "wiggle room" to be held responsible.
