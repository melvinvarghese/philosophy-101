# Materialism vs. Dualism: What Are We Made Of?

This is the oldest fight in the book.

## The Materialists (Team Matter)
They say: **Everything is physical.**
Your thoughts? Just chemical reactions. Your love? Dopamine. Your soul? Doesn't exist. The universe is just atoms bumping into each other.

## The Dualists (Team Spirit)
They say: **There are two things: Matter and Mind.**
Your body is a machine, but "You" are the ghost in the machine. Even if you cloned your body perfectly, the clone wouldn't be *you* because it lacks your soul/consciousness.

### Reflection
*If scientists could upload your brain to a computer, would you still be you? Or just a copy? Your answer tells you which team you're on.*
