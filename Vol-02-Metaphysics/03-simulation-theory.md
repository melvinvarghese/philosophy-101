# The Simulation Theory: Are We in a Video Game?

It sounds like science fiction, but it’s serious philosophy (modern metaphysics).

**The Argument (Nick Bostrom):**
1. Computers get faster every year.
2. Eventually, we will be able to simulate entire universes.
3. If we can, we will probably run millions of simulations (to study history, sociology, etc.).
4. Therefore, there are millions of "fake" universes and only one "real" base reality.
5. **Statistically, you are probably in one of the millions of fakes.**

### Reflection
*If you found out this was a simulation, would you live your life differently? Would you stop paying taxes?*
