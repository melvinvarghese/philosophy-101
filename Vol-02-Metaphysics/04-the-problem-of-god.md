# The Problem of God

Philosophy doesn't tell you what to believe, but it analyzes the arguments.

**The Ontological Argument:** God is "that than which nothing greater can be conceived." Since existing is greater than not existing, God must exist. (A bit of a brain twister).

**The Cosmological Argument:** Everything has a cause. The Universe must have a cause. That "First Cause" is God.

**The Problem of Evil:** If God is All-Good and All-Powerful, why do innocent kids get sick?
* If He can't stop it, He isn't All-Powerful.
* If He won't stop it, He isn't All-Good.
