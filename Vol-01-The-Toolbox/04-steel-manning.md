# Steel-Manning: The Ultimate Power Move

Most people "Straw Man" their opponents (make them look dumb). Strong thinkers "Steel Man" them.

**The Steel Man:** You express your opponent's argument *better* than they did. You help them build the strongest possible version of their case. AND THEN you argue against it.

If you can defeat the Steel Man, you have actually won. If you only defeat the Straw Man, you've accomplished nothing.

### Reflection
*Pick an opinion you hate. Try to write a paragraph defending it so well that a believer would say, "Yes, exactly!"*
