# Logical Fallacies: Part 1

A fallacy is a bug in your brain's software. Here are the big ones:

1.  **Ad Hominem:** Attacking the person, not the idea. "You're wrong because you're ugly." (Obviously bad logic, but we do it all the time).
2.  **Straw Man:** Attacking a weak version of the argument. "You hate cars? So you want us all to ride horses?" No, I just want better trains.
3.  **Slippery Slope:** Assuming A leads to Z. "If we allow sad movies, everyone will get depressed and society will collapse."

### Reflection
*Catch yourself using a 'Straw Man' today. Did you actually listen to your friend, or did you just argue against a caricature of them?*
