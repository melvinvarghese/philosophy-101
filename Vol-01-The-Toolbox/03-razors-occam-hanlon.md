# Mental Razors: Occam and Hanlon

A "Razor" is a mental tool that cuts away unnecessary noise.

## Occam’s Razor
"The simplest explanation is usually the right one."
If your keys are missing, did a ghost hide them, or did you drop them? Ghost theories require too many assumptions. You probably just dropped them.

## Hanlon’s Razor
"Never attribute to malice that which is adequately explained by stupidity."
If someone cuts you off in traffic, they probably aren't "out to get you." They are probably just a bad driver or distracted. Don't take it personally.

### Reflection
*How much anger could you save if you assumed people were just clumsy/forgetful rather than evil?*
