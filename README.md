# Philosophy 101

Welcome to the rabbit hole.


## How to Navigate

The library is divided into **10 Volumes**.

* [**Vol 1: The Toolbox**](/Vol-01-The-Toolbox) - Start here. Logic, arguing, and how to think.
* [**Vol 2: Metaphysics**](/Vol-02-Metaphysics) - The weird stuff. God, simulations, and reality.
* [**Vol 3: Epistemology**](https://github.com/melvinvarghese/philosophy-101/tree/main/Vol-03-Epistemology) - Knowledge. Truth, science, and skepticism.
* [**Vol 4: The Mind**](https://github.com/melvinvarghese/philosophy-101/tree/main/Vol-04-The-Mind) - Consciousness, AI, and the Self.
* [**Vol 5: Ethics**](https://github.com/melvinvarghese/philosophy-101/tree/main/Vol-05-Ethics) - Right, wrong, and the gray areas.
* [**Vol 6: Politics**](https://github.com/melvinvarghese/philosophy-101/tree/main/Vol-06-Politics) - Power, justice, and freedom.
* [**Vol 7: Aesthetics**](https://github.com/melvinvarghese/philosophy-101/tree/main/Vol-07-Aesthetics) - Art, beauty, and horror.
* [**Vol 8: The Ancients**](https://github.com/melvinvarghese/philosophy-101/tree/main/Vol-08-The-Ancients) - Where it all began (Greeks, Romans, etc.).
* [**Vol 9: The Moderns**](https://github.com/melvinvarghese/philosophy-101/tree/main/Vol-09-The-Moderns) - The thinkers who built the modern world.
* [**Vol 10: The Search for Meaning**](https://github.com/melvinvarghese/philosophy-101/tree/main/Vol-10-Meaning-of-Life) - Existentialism, Nihilism, and Eastern Wisdom.

## A Note on Reading

Don't try to read this cover to cover. Treat it like a buffet.
* Feeling lost? Go to **Vol 10**.
* Feeling angry at the news? Go to **Vol 6**.
* Feeling confused about reality? Go to **Vol 2**.

Enjoy the journey.
