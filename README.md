# Philosophy 101: The Encyclopedia of Life, Mind & Meaning

Welcome to the rabbit hole.

You have just downloaded a massive archive of human thought. This isn't a textbook; it's a toolkit. It covers over **100 concepts**, from the ancient Greeks arguing in the market square to modern bioethicists worrying about designer babies.

We’ve written this in plain English. No Latin (unless we translate it). No gatekeeping. Just the raw ideas that have shaped human history, explained as if we were having coffee.

## How to Navigate this Encyclopedia

The library is divided into **10 Volumes**.

* [**Vol 1: The Toolbox**](/Vol-01-The-Toolbox) - Start here. Logic, arguing, and how to think.
* **Vol 2: Metaphysics** - The weird stuff. God, simulations, and reality.
* **Vol 3: Epistemology** - Knowledge. Truth, science, and skepticism.
* **Vol 4: The Mind** - Consciousness, AI, and the Self.
* **Vol 5: Ethics** - Right, wrong, and the gray areas.
* **Vol 6: Politics** - Power, justice, and freedom.
* **Vol 7: Aesthetics** - Art, beauty, and horror.
* **Vol 8: The Ancients** - Where it all began (Greeks, Romans, etc.).
* **Vol 9: The Moderns** - The thinkers who built the modern world.
* **Vol 10: The Search for Meaning** - Existentialism, Nihilism, and Eastern Wisdom.

## A Note on Reading

Don't try to read this cover to cover. Treat it like a buffet.
* Feeling lost? Go to **Vol 10**.
* Feeling angry at the news? Go to **Vol 6**.
* Feeling confused about reality? Go to **Vol 2**.

Enjoy the journey.
