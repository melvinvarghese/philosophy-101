# Existentialism: Sartre and Camus

**Sartre:** "Existence precedes Essence."
You have no blueprint. You are terrifyingly free. If you ask "What should I do?" the only answer is "Choose."
**Bad Faith:** Lying to yourself that you have no choice ("I *have* to work this job"). No, you choose to work it because you want money. Own the choice.

**Camus:** The Absurd.
Life is meaningless. The universe is silent.
Should we cry? No. We should revolt.
"One must imagine Sisyphus happy." Enjoy the struggle. The meaning of life is whatever you're doing right now.

### Reflection
*When do you make excuses for your life? "I can't leave because..." Sartre calls that Bad Faith.*
