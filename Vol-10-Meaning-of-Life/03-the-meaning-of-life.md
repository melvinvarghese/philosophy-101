# The Meaning of Life (Summary)

So, what is the meaning of life? We've looked at 100 ideas. Here are the options:

1.  **Nihilism:** There is no meaning. (Freedom).
2.  **Theism:** To serve God/The Divine. (Duty).
3.  **Existentialism:** To create your own meaning. (Creativity).
4.  **Hedonism:** To enjoy the ride. (Pleasure).
5.  **Humanism:** To help others and advance the species. (Community).
6.  **Absurdism:** To rebel against the meaninglessness. (Spite).

Philosophy doesn't give you the answer. It gives you the menu. You have to order.

### Reflection
*If you died today, what would you want written on your tombstone? Not your job title. Your essence.*
