# Eastern Wisdom: Letting Go

**Buddhism:**
Life is Dukkha (Dissatisfaction).
The cause is Tanha (Craving/Clinging).
The Self is an illusion (*Anatta*).
Stop trying to hold onto the river. Let it flow.

**Taoism:**
The Tao that can be spoken is not the eternal Tao.
**Wu Wei:** Effortless action.
Don't fight the current. Be like water. The stiff tree breaks in the storm; the flexible bamboo survives.

**Advaita Vedanta (Hinduism):**
Atman is Brahman.
Your soul (Atman) is identical to the universal soul (Brahman). You are the universe experiencing itself. Separation is an illusion.

### Reflection
*Western philosophy tries to FIX the world. Eastern philosophy tries to ACCEPT it. Which approach do you need right now?*
