# The Social Contract: Why We Have Laws

Hobbes, Locke, and Rousseau asked: Why do we let governments boss us around?

**The State of Nature:**
Imagine a world without laws. Hobbes said it would be "nasty, brutish, and short." Total war.

**The Deal:**
We sign a theoretical "Social Contract."
* **We give up:** Absolute freedom (I can't just kill you for your shoes).
* **We gain:** Security (The police will stop you from killing me).

If the government stops protecting us, the contract is void, and we have the right to revolt (this idea inspired the American Revolution).
