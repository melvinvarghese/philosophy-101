# Two Concepts of Liberty

Isaiah Berlin split freedom into two types:

**1. Negative Liberty (Freedom FROM):**
* "Leave me alone."
* The government doesn't stop you from speaking, traveling, or buying things.
* Example: The US Bill of Rights.

**2. Positive Liberty (Freedom TO):**
* "Empower me."
* Having the actual *capacity* to do things. You aren't "free" to go to college if you're broke. You aren't "free" to stay healthy if you can't afford a doctor.
* This justifies social welfare, public schools, etc.

Political arguments usually happen because one side wants Negative Liberty and the other wants Positive Liberty.

### Reflection
*Are you truly free if you are homeless? Nobody is stopping you from buying a house (Negative Liberty), but you can't do it (Positive Liberty).*
