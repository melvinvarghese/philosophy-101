# Marxism 101: Class Struggle

Forget the Cold War for a second. As a philosophy, Marxism is a way of analyzing history through money.

**Historical Materialism:**
Marx said history isn't driven by great ideas or kings; it's driven by who owns the factories (Capital) and who works in them (Labor).

**Alienation:**
Workers are miserable because they are treated like cogs. You make a chair, but you don't own it; the boss sells it. You are disconnected from your own work.

Marx predicted that eventually, the workers would realize their power, overthrow the owners, and share the resources (Communism).
