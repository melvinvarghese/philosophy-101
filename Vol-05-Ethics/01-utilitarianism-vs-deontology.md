# The Heavyweights: Outcome vs. Duty

**Utilitarianism (Mill, Bentham):**
The logic of the Greater Good.
* **Rule:** Maximize happiness, minimize suffering.
* **Scenario:** If killing one innocent person cures cancer for millions, do it. The math works out.

**Deontology (Kant):**
The logic of Duty and Rules.
* **Rule:** Some things are inherently wrong, no matter the outcome.
* **Scenario:** You cannot kill the innocent person. Period. Even if it saves the world. People have rights that cannot be calculated away.

### Reflection
*Most of us are Utilitarians about tax policy, but Deontologists about our own families. Why the switch?*
