# Moral Relativism: Is Morality Just Opinion?

**Moral Relativism:** "What is right for you might not be right for me."
It argues that morality is just cultural. The Spartans threw weak babies off cliffs. We think that's evil. They thought it was necessary. Who is objectively right?

**The Danger:**
If morality is *only* relative, then we can't say the Nazis were "wrong." We can only say "we disagreed with them." Most philosophers hate this conclusion, so they search for objective moral truths.

### Reflection
*Are there any moral rules that should apply to every human on earth, regardless of their culture? (e.g., Don't torture children?)*
