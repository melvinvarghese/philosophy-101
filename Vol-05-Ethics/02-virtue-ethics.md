# Virtue Ethics: Be a Good Person

Aristotle didn't care about ethical formulas. He cared about character.

He said: "Don't ask 'What should I do?' Ask 'What kind of person should I be?'"
If you cultivate virtues—like Courage, Honesty, Kindness—you will naturally do the right thing when the time comes.

**The Golden Mean:**
Virtue is always the middle path between two extremes.
* Cowardice < **Courage** > Recklessness
* Stinginess < **Generosity** > Wastefulness

### Reflection
*Who is the most virtuous person you know? What makes them that way? It probably isn't because they memorize rules.*
