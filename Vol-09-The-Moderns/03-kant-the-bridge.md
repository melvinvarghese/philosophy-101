# Immanuel Kant: The System Builder

Kant looked at the Rationalists and Empiricists and said, "You're both half-right."

**Transcendental Idealism:**
We can't know the world "in itself" (*Noumena*). We can only know the world as it appears to us (*Phenomena*).
Our brains have built-in "glasses" (Space, Time, Causality). We can't see reality without these glasses.

He merged the mind and the world. We don't just record reality; we construct it.

### Reflection
*The world looks very different to a bat using sonar. Is their world less "real" than yours? Kant would say we are both just trapped in our own perceptions.*
