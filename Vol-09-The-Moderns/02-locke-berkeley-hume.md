# The Empiricists: Experience is King

**John Locke:**
Father of Liberalism. Said we have "Natural Rights" (Life, Liberty, Property). His ideas are the software running the USA.

**George Berkeley:**
"To be is to be perceived."
If a tree falls in a forest and no one is there, it doesn't exist. Reality is just ideas in the mind of God. (Mind-bending Idealism).

**David Hume:**
The Great Skeptic. Said we can't even prove that the sun will rise tomorrow (The Problem of Induction). Just because it happened a million times doesn't mean it *must* happen again. He woke everyone up.

### Reflection
*Hume says we feel things more than we think them. "Reason is the slave of the passions." Do you agree?*
