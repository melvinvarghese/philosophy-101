# Friedrich Nietzsche: Dynamite

Nietzsche is often misunderstood as a Nazi (he wasn't; his sister was). He was a diagnostic philosopher.

**"God is Dead":**
He didn't mean he killed God. He meant that science and modern life have made religion unbelievable for most people. This creates a vacuum: **Nihilism**.

**The Ubermensch (Overman):**
To survive the death of God, we must become gods ourselves. We must create our *own* values. We must say "Yes" to life, even the suffering.

**Amor Fati:**
"Love your fate." Don't just tolerate your life; love it. Every mistake, every pain. Own it.

### Reflection
*Could you live your life over and over again, exactly the same way, for eternity? If not, change it.*
