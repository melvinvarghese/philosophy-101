# The Rationalists: Math and God

**Descartes:**
"I think, therefore I am." Dualist. Tried to prove God exists using logic.

**Spinoza:**
The ultimate rebel. Said God *is* the Universe. Nature = God. He denied miracles and free will. He was excommunicated, but Einstein loved his view of God.

**Leibniz:**
Super-genius who invented Calculus (independent of Newton). Claimed we live in the "Best of All Possible Worlds" because God wouldn't make a crappy one. (Voltaire made fun of him for this).
