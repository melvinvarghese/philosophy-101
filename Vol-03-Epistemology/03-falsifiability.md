# Science and Falsifiability

Karl Popper changed how we do science. He said: **"Scientific theories must be falsifiable."**

If I say, "There is an invisible, undetectable dragon in my garage," you can't prove me wrong. But that's not science; that's nonsense.
For a theory to be scientific, there must be a way to test it and potentially fail.

* **Bad Science:** "My horoscope is always right" (Vague, can't be disproven).
* **Good Science:** "Water boils at 100°C" (Specific, you can test it to see if I'm wrong).

### Reflection
*Are you holding onto any beliefs that are "unfalsifiable"—beliefs that no amount of evidence could ever change?*
