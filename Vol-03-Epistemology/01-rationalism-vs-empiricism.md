# Rationalism vs. Empiricism: The Source of Truth

How do you know things?

## The Rationalists (Descartes, Plato)
**"Use your brain."**
They believe we can figure out truth just by thinking hard. Math is a great example. You don't need to go outside to know 2+2=4. You just *know* it logically. They trust logic over eyes (because eyes can be tricked).

## The Empiricists (Locke, Hume)
**"Use your eyes."**
They believe we are born as "Blank Slates" (*Tabula Rasa*). We know nothing until we see, touch, or taste it. You only know fire is hot because you touched it once.

### Reflection
*Are you more likely to trust a scientific study (Empiricism) or your own logical gut feeling (Rationalism)?*
