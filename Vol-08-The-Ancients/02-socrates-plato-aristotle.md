# The Big Three: Socrates, Plato, Aristotle

The holy trinity of Greek thought.

**1. Socrates:**
The street philosopher. Asked annoying questions ("What is justice?"). Admitted he knew nothing. Was executed for "corrupting the youth" (teaching them to question authority).

**2. Plato (Socrates' Student):**
The Idealist. Believed this world is a shadow of a perfect "World of Forms." Wrote *The Republic* (a design for a perfect, slightly authoritarian city).

**3. Aristotle (Plato's Student):**
The Realist. Disagreed with Plato. Said truth is found *in* the world, not in some heaven. Invented Biology, Logic, and Taxonomy. He categorized *everything*.

### Reflection
*Are you a Platonist (dreamer, looking for perfection) or an Aristotelian (scientist, looking at the data)?*
