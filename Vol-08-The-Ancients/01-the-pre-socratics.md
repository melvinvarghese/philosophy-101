# The Pre-Socratics: The First Scientists

Before Socrates, there were guys trying to figure out nature without saying "Zeus did it."

* **Thales:** Thought everything was made of Water. (Wrong, but a good guess).
* **Heraclitus:** "You cannot step in the same river twice." Everything is constant change (Flux).
* **Parmenides:** "Change is an illusion." Nothing actually changes; our senses just lie to us.
* **Democritus:** Guessed that the world was made of tiny, uncuttable "atoms." He was weirdly right.

They invented the idea of "Laws of Nature."
