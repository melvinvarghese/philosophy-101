# Epicureanism: It's Not Just About Food

Today, an "Epicure" is someone who likes fancy wine. But Epicurus was different.
He was a **Hedonist**, but a smart one.

He believed the goal of life is Pleasure (Absence of Pain).
BUT, wild partying leads to hangovers (Pain). So, the best life is simple.
* Bread and water.
* Good friends.
* Quiet conversation.
* No fear of gods or death.

"Death is nothing to us. When we exist, death is not; and when death exists, we are not."

### Reflection
*What "pleasures" in your life actually cause you more pain in the long run (e.g., doomscrolling, junk food)?*
