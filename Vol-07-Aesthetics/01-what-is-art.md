# What is Art? (The Toilet Problem)

In 1917, Marcel Duchamp took a porcelain urinal, signed it "R. Mutt," and put it in an art gallery. He called it *Fountain*.

It caused a meltdown.
* **Traditionalists:** "This isn't art! It requires no skill! It's a toilet!"
* **Modernists:** "It is art because the artist *chose* it. Art is about the idea, not the craft."

This birthed **Conceptual Art**. It forces us to ask: Is art about beauty? Skill? Or just intent?

### Reflection
*If a monkey paints a canvas by accident, is it art? What if a human sells it for a million dollars?*
