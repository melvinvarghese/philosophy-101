# The Sublime: Beauty that Terrifies

Edmund Burke distinguished between the **Beautiful** and the **Sublime**.

* **Beautiful:** Small, smooth, pleasant. A flower. A puppy. It makes us feel safe.
* **Sublime:** Massive, jagged, dangerous. A thunderstorm. The Grand Canyon. The vast ocean.

The Sublime makes us feel tiny. It is a mix of awe and terror. It reminds us of our insignificance in the universe, which is a weirdly spiritual feeling.

### Reflection
*When was the last time you looked at something so big it made you feel small?*
