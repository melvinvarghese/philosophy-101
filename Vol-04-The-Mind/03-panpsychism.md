# Panpsychism: Is Everything Conscious?

This is a wild one, but it's making a comeback.
Materialism struggles to explain how "dead" atoms create "alive" consciousness.

**Panpsychism suggests:** Maybe consciousness is a fundamental property of matter, like gravity.
Maybe an electron has a tiny, tiny bit of consciousness. A rock has a bit more. A brain has a lot, organized into a complex mind.

It sounds crazy, but it solves the problem of "where does consciousness come from?" It says: It was there all along.
