# The Chinese Room: Can AI Understand?

Imagine a man locked in a room. He doesn't speak Chinese.
He has a rule book: "If you see symbol X, write symbol Y."
People slide Chinese questions under the door. He follows the book and slides answers back.
To the people outside, it looks like he speaks fluent Chinese.
But does he? No. He's just manipulating symbols.

**John Searle's Argument:**
Computers (and AI like ChatGPT) are the man in the room. They manipulate symbols perfectly, but they don't *understand* what they are saying. They have syntax (rules), but no semantics (meaning).
