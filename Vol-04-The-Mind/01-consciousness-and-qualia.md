# Consciousness and Qualia

**Qualia** (pronounced kwah-lee-ah) is the raw feeling of experience.
It is the *redness* of a rose. The *pain* of a headache. The *taste* of wine.

You can explain to a blind person the physics of light waves. You can describe how the eye sends signals to the brain. But they will never know what "Red" looks like.
That missing piece? That's Qualia.

It suggests that science can explain the *machinery* of the brain, but maybe not the *feeling* of being alive.
